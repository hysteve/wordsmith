## Goldmine

Why goldmine? What does a goldmine have to do with search ranking scores?

From what I can tell, this set of scores is primarily related to analyzing and scoring page titles. Titles are especially important to Google since they appear on Google's website (possibly not in all cases) as the link you click when you navigate to a search result.

More importantly is what the goldmine properties represent - a set of result page title ranking scores and scoring factors, giving some comcrete insight into what Google is paying attention to.

`goldmineBlockbertFactor`
"Google may use Blockbert, a machine learning model, to assess the title's fluency, coherence, and relevance, which could contribute to the overall title quality score."

`goldmineSitenameFactor`
"Google might use this signal to prioritize titles that include the website's name, which could help users recognize and trust the source of the information."

Takeaway: Put your websiute name in the title

`goldmineSiteQueryFactor`
"Google might analyze the relationship between the website's content and the user's search query to determine the relevance of the title."
"Directly improves search quality by prioritizing titles that are relevant to the website and user query."

Takeaway: Put the query you are optimizing for in the title

`goldmineOgTitleFactor`
"Directly improves search quality by considering the OG title as a potential title option."
"Google may use the OG title, which is specifically designed for social media sharing, as a secondary signal to understand the page's content and relevance to the user's query."

Takeaway: Always use opengraph.

`goldmineBodyFactor`
"Google might use natural language processing techniques to determine how well the title reflects the main topics and keywords used in the body of the content."

Takeaway: Revolve the content and title around a target query, and build out keywords and text from there.

`goldmineSalientTermFactor`
"Google might identify and emphasize salient terms in the title that match the user's search query, which could improve the visibility and click-through rate of the result."

Here, "salient terms" could be interpreted as "direct hit keyword", as in keyword phrases (N-grams) that were in both the search query and (in this case) in the title.

Takeaway: Put the query you are optimizing for in the title (this is the 2nd time, must be important)

`goldmineTrustFactor`
"Google might use this signal to prioritize titles from websites with a good reputation and history of providing accurate and reliable information."

Takeaway: "Trust" is a vague concept so far from Google, but definitely important (sort of like "authority")

`goldmineIsTitleTag`, `goldmineTitleTagFactor`
"Google may prioritize titles extracted directly from the title tag, which is typically considered the most accurate representation of a page's content."

Takeaway: Don't forget a title tag
